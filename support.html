Frequently asked

Does VeriSieve store my social media passwords?

No. You sign in to each platform through its own website inside the app's built-in browser, exactly as you would in Safari — your credentials go directly to that platform, never to us.

Which platforms does VeriSieve support?

X (Twitter), BlueSky, Instagram, Reddit, Facebook, and TikTok.

Is my data sent to a server?

Almost everything runs on-device. The one exception: when you explicitly tap "Fact Check" on a post, that post's text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.
